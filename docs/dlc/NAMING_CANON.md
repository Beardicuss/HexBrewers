# Expansion Naming Canon

Status: draft canon pending creator approval.

## Mirewrought Pacts

- Internal compatibility ID: `covenants_of_the_mire`
- Gameplay link: Mire Covenants, Covenant Seals, Echo Reed, Embercore, Abyssal Runoff.
- Meaning: bargains shaped by the sentient mire surrounding Ashenveil rather than a
  generic circle of witches.
- Avoid publicly using the retired working title “Covenants of the Mire.”

## Veilglass Afflictions

- Internal compatibility ID: `distillers_of_the_veil`
- Gameplay link: Affliction Contracts, Veil Retorts, Distillate Markers, Remedy Paths.
- Meaning: supernatural maladies become visible as fractures and vapor inside alchemical
  veilglass, allowing hexbrewers to distill their symptoms into power.
- Avoid publicly using the retired working title “Distillers of the Veil.”

Internal IDs remain unchanged to preserve existing local saves and tests. They are not
storefront or player-facing names.
