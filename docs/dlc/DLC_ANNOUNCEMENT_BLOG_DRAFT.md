# Two Forbidden Paths Are Opening in Ashenveil

Status: website-news draft. The expansions are in active development and are not yet

## Suggested excerpt

Two optional expansions are being forged for *Hexbrewers of Ashenveil*. **Mirewrought
Pacts** introduces dangerous Covenants, a fifth brewer, new ingredients, and brewing
beyond the crucible's known limits. **Veilglass Afflictions** adds supernatural patients,
persistent remedy tracks, and a new Distillation phase. Both expansions are designed to
work independently or together, while preserving the complete base-game experience.

## The laboratory is no longer sealed

Ashenveil has always rewarded brewers willing to place one more ingredient into an
unstable crucible. Our first two expansions push that temptation in different directions.
One asks what you are willing to promise for immediate power. The other asks whether a
curse can be understood by reducing it, drop by luminous drop, into its purest form.

These are optional content packs. Players will be able to enable either expansion, use
both together, or continue playing the unchanged base game. The game now includes a
dedicated DLC screen where each pack's mechanics and story can be reviewed before it is
enabled for a new match.

## Mirewrought Pacts

*The mire remembers every promise.*

Beneath the oldest laboratory district, roots have split the stone and exposed a black
mire that speaks in the voices of forgotten brewers. It offers three kinds of bargains:
Bone, Moon, and Blood. The Hollow has already accepted its invitation. Every other
hexbrewer must decide which promises are worth carrying into the crucible.

### What it adds

- A fifth player position and a new AI rival, **The Hollow**.
- Twelve original **Mire Covenants**, divided between Bone, Moon, and Blood.
- One matching Covenant Seal for each category, usable once per match.
- **Echo Reed**, a variable-distance ingredient whose movement responds to match state.
- **Embercore-6**, a powerful high-value ingredient.
- **Abyssal Runoff**, which allows a brew to continue producing rewards beyond the
  normal end of the crucible track.
- Two additional complete ingredient-book configurations: recipe sets 5 and 6.
- New decisions for AI opponents, market valuation, saving, localization, and the Grimoire.

Mirewrought Pacts is about concentrated moments of power. A Covenant can rescue a spent
vial, move the starting droplet, provide emergency buying power, or reshape a losing
round—but every seal works only once. Saving a pact too long can be as costly as invoking
it too early.

The expansion also changes the physical rhythm of brewing. Echo Reed rewards awareness
of the current match, Embercore creates enormous leaps around the crucible, and Abyssal
Runoff turns excess movement into a scoring opportunity instead of a dead end.

## Veilglass Afflictions

*Every malady leaves a shape in the glass.*

A pale sickness is moving through Ashenveil without touching skin. Its victims dream
other people's memories, cast reflections that refuse to obey, and exhale ash inside
sealed rooms. Ordinary physicians see no cause. Hexbrewers see fractures suspended in
veilglass—and a dangerous possibility for treatment.

### What it adds

- Eight original **Affliction Contracts**, each with its own symptom and Remedy Path.
- A new end-of-round **Distillation phase**.
- Persistent **Veil Retorts** that collect Distillate across multiple rounds.
- Milestone rewards including Prestige and head starts for a later brew.
- New Veilglass-specific Omens integrated into the existing Omen deck.
- Two distinct Echo Reed formulas: **Retort Resonance** and **Deep Resonance**.
- Dedicated AI resolution, save support, localized interface text, and Grimoire entries.

Veilglass Afflictions adds a longer strategic arc to every match. Ingredient diversity,
the condition of neighboring crucibles, and the chosen Affliction determine how quickly
a Retort advances. Remedy rewards then feed back into future rounds, making early
distillation choices matter long after the market closes.

Each Affliction emphasizes a different progression pattern. Some provide reliable
Prestige at several thresholds; others exchange a slower cure for a stronger brewing
head start. Players can see the path in advance and decide how much of the current round
to sacrifice for a future remedy.

## Designed to combine

Both packs share the same modular rules foundation. When used together, Covenant timing,
Abyssal Runoff, Echo Reed formulas, Affliction progress, and the Distillation phase remain
separate systems with explicit timing. This lets the expansions interact without making
the base rules ambiguous.

The base game remains available without either pack. Expansion-only ingredients, recipe
sets, player counts, Omens, setup decisions, and phase rules are gated behind their
corresponding DLC toggle.

## Development status

Core systems for both expansions are currently playable in local development builds.
Rules implementation, AI behavior, save compatibility, localization, automated matches,
and combined-pack testing are underway. Balance values, final visual assets, audio,
storefront details, pricing, and release timing are not final.

We will share more as the laboratory work continues—including closer looks at the twelve
Mire Covenants, all eight Affliction Contracts, final artwork, and release plans.
