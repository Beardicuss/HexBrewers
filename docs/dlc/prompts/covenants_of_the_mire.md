# Art Brief — Mirewrought Pacts

Version: 2026-10-17-v1

## Shared style lock

Dark fantasy alchemical laboratory illustration, ancient wet stone, tarnished brass,
blackened silver, violet potion light, restrained turquoise bioluminescence, painterly
realism, intricate but readable silhouette, cinematic low-key lighting, subtle film grain.
Original Hexbrewers universe. No typography, logos, board-game components, borders, copied
characters, modern objects, cartoon rendering, or recognizable third-party iconography.

## Key art prompt

An underground covenant chamber branching from the Ashenveil laboratory. Three original
ritual alcoves represent bone, moon, and blood through material and lighting rather than
literal labels. A lone brewer holds three distinct seals while an eerie reed-like magical
ingredient echoes the colors of nearby potions. A cracked stone runoff channel disappears
into a bottomless violet abyss. Wide 16:9 composition, clear negative space for UI on the
left, focal action on the right, consistent perspective, no text.

## The Hollow portrait prompt

Original masked rival brewer called The Hollow, androgynous silhouette, weathered charcoal
robes, porcelain mask fractured into an empty dark center, tiny turquoise reflections and
one violet reagent vial, shoulders-up portrait, strong readable silhouette, dark neutral
background, no text, no existing franchise resemblance.

## Covenant portrait system

Generate twelve original supernatural patrons as separate portraits:

- Bone: ossuary materials, ivory/umber palette, dry particulate magic.
- Moon: oxidized silver, indigo/turquoise palette, cold vapor and lensing.
- Blood: dark crimson glass, black iron, restrained liquid glow without gore.

Each category needs four unmistakably different silhouettes. Keep camera, border-safe area,
contrast, and face scale consistent across the set. Do not use stereotypical pointed-hat
witch imagery.

## Ingredient prompts

**Echo Reed:** a single crooked marsh reed grown through a translucent alchemical crystal,
concentric spectral echoes along its stem, readable circular-token silhouette, transparent
background, top-down three-quarter view, no text.

**Embercore 6:** a dense orange mineral seed with six subtle radial fissures, internal
heat beneath soot-black shell, readable at 48 px, transparent background, no numeral or text.

## Review checklist

- Compare against existing `main_menu_bg.jpg`, ingredient icons, and avatar crop.
- Reject third-party resemblance, embedded letters, illegible token silhouettes, excessive
  neon saturation, flat mobile-game rendering, and impossible object geometry.
- Save accepted prompt, seed, model, and edit notes beside every master.
