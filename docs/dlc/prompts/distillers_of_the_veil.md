# Art Brief — Veilglass Afflictions

Version: 2026-10-17-v1

## Shared style lock

Dark fantasy medical alchemy inside Ashenveil: cathedral laboratory stone, hand-blown glass,
tarnished brass tubes, spectral turquoise vapor, violet reagents, painterly realism, low-key
cinematic light, unsettling but not grotesque. Original worldbuilding only. No typography,
logos, copied illnesses/characters, modern medical equipment, cartoon style, or third-party
board/card layouts.

## Key art prompt

A towering branching retort distills luminous vapor upward from several potion vessels.
Three anonymous afflicted travelers wait behind translucent curtains while an original
brewer calibrates brass valves. Vapor forms abstract, ambiguous symptoms rather than faces.
Wide 16:9 composition, central vertical flow from table to ceiling, left-side negative space
for storefront title treatment, no text.

## Affliction Contract system

Create eight original afflicted figures, each defined by a supernatural condition expressed
through lighting, posture, clothing, and a unique emblem. Avoid the reference expansion's
named patients and ailments. Suggested original directions:

1. Ash Sleep — drifting cinders freeze around a sleepless archivist.
2. Glass Pulse — crystalline light fractures across a courier's hands.
3. Borrowed Shadow — a figure's shadow faces the wrong direction.
4. Tidemarked — damp constellations move beneath a sailor's cloak.
5. Bell Breath — concentric brass ripples emerge with each exhale.
6. Lantern Hunger — dim mothlike lights orbit an empty chest lantern.
7. Thorn Memory — spectral vines knot around forgotten keepsakes.
8. Mirror Fever — reflections lag behind the subject by one gesture.

Portraits share framing and contrast but must have distinct silhouettes. Symptoms should be
mysterious, non-graphic, and readable at thumbnail size.

## Retort and marker prompts

**Veil Retort:** wide transparent UI ornament, symmetrical antique glass-and-brass apparatus,
seven clear progress chambers connected by readable tubes, restrained glow, empty center
space for code-rendered numbers/icons, no text, front orthographic presentation.

**Distillate Marker:** suspended teardrop of turquoise vapor sealed in a black-silver ring,
high contrast at 32 px, transparent background, no text.

## VFX brief

Upward-moving vapor begins as narrow wisps at the table, joins into asymmetric lobes, curls
through the retort, and thins near the ceiling. Use alpha masks or a sprite atlas suitable for
additive/normal blending. Avoid a straight column, hard rectangular edges, and uniform speed.

## Review checklist

- Maintain the base game's teal/violet balance without obscuring UI.
- Reject anatomical errors, copied card framing, embedded text, excessive horror/gore,
  uniform smoke columns, and details that vanish below 96 px.
- Save accepted prompt, seed, model, and edit notes beside every master.
