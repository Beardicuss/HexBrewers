# DLC Story Canon — Draft

Status: creator-review draft. These entries support the in-game DLC screen and are not
final canon until approved.

## Mirewrought Pacts

- Location: beneath the oldest laboratory district of Ashenveil.
- Inciting event: ancient roots break the laboratory floor and expose a sentient black mire.
- Supernatural rule: the mire speaks with the voices of forgotten brewers and remembers
  every promise made to it.
- Player connection: each hexbrewer binds one Bone, one Moon, and one Blood Covenant.
- Character connection: The Hollow has already answered the mire before the player arrives.
- Open mystery: the true price of the Covenants is deliberately unknown.

In-game chronicle:

> Beneath Ashenveil, roots have broken the oldest laboratory floor. Through the cracks
> rises a black mire that speaks in the voices of forgotten brewers, offering power in
> exchange for promises it refuses to name.
>
> The Hollow has already answered. Now every hexbrewer must choose three seals—Bone,
> Moon, and Blood—and learn whether a pact is a tool, a debt, or merely a slower kind
> of curse.

Gameplay links: Covenant selection, Covenant Seals, The Hollow, Echo Reed, Embercore-6,
Abyssal Runoff, and recipe sets 5–6.

## Veilglass Afflictions

- Location: Ashenveil and its cathedral laboratories.
- Inciting event: a supernatural sickness crosses the city without ordinary physical contact.
- Symptoms: shared dreams, independently moving reflections, and ash exhaled inside sealed rooms.
- Supernatural rule: each illness appears as a visible fracture within veilglass.
- Player connection: hexbrewers distil symptoms into essence to advance a Remedy Path.
- Open mystery: filling a Veil Retort allows something beyond Ashenveil to look back.

In-game chronicle:

> A pale sickness has crossed Ashenveil without touching skin. Victims dream one
> another's memories, cast reflections that move alone, and breathe ash into sealed
> rooms. Ordinary physicians call it impossible.
>
> The hexbrewers see fractures inside veilglass. By distilling each symptom into
> luminous essence, they may uncover a cure—but every filled retort brings the unseen
> side of Ashenveil closer to looking back.

Gameplay links: Affliction Contracts, Veil Retorts, Distillate, Remedy Paths,
Veilglass Omens, Retort Resonance, and Deep Resonance.

## Continuity notes

- Neither expansion names or imitates characters, locations, or story material from a
  reference board game.
- Ashenveil remains the English place name. Existing Russian and Georgian localizations
  retain their established localized forms.
- The Hollow remains mysterious; this draft does not define identity, origin, gender,
  or allegiance.
- The unseen side of Ashenveil is intentionally unnamed so later story work can define it.
